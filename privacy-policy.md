# Privacy Policy: AwayDays

**Last updated: October 2026**

## Overview

AwayDays is a Chrome extension that counts down to your next annual leave and helps you prepare with a pre-leave checklist. This policy explains how the extension handles your data.

## Data Collected

AwayDays stores the following data **in your browser only**:

- The names and dates of the leave you add
- The type of holiday you choose for each trip
- Your checklist items and whether they are ticked
- Your appearance choice (Auto, Light or Dark)

No other data is stored. Countdowns and working day counts are calculated on the fly from your system clock and are never saved.

## Data Storage

Data is stored using Chrome's `storage.sync` API. If Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome may sync this data between your own signed-in browsers; this is handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If Chrome sync is turned off, the data stays on your device only.

This data is not accessible to any website, third party or the developer. Finished trips are removed automatically two weeks after they end, and uninstalling the extension removes all of its data.

## Data Sharing

No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with any external server, service, or third party.

AwayDays does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising, and does not load any code from the internet.

## Permissions

- **storage** - used to save your trips, checklist and appearance choice in your browser
- **alarms** - used to refresh the days-left number on the toolbar icon every 30 minutes
